About me

I'm Jaan, born and raised in Greymouth. I've been painting and working on ropes for over ten years.

I hold a rope access ticket, so I can get to steep roofs and high walls without scaffolding, which saves you money. I do the work myself, from the quote to the last coat, and I make sure it's done right.

Common questions

How do quotes work?

Quotes are free. Tell us what the job is and where it is, using the form below, a phone call or an email, and Jaan will get back to you.

How does rope access avoid scaffolding?

Jaan works at height from ropes, so there's no need to hire and put up scaffolding for roofs and high walls. That saves you the cost of scaffolding.

What areas do you cover?

Greymouth, Westport, Reefton, Hokitika and across the West Coast.

What do you paint?

Roofs, houses, sheds and commercial buildings. We paint anything.

Do you guarantee your work?

Our workmanship is guaranteed. On old, rusty roofs that have had problems before, like peeling, we can't guarantee how the paint will perform.
